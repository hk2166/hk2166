### DESCRIPTION

I'm a CS undergrad at Newton School of Technology (2024–2028), based in Pune. I work across the whole stack, from the Raspberry Pi inside a robot to the web app on top of it.

Two ideas keep pulling me back. The first is **AI that runs on your own machine**, with no cloud bill and no data leaving the laptop. The second is **agents that do real work on someone's behalf**.

Open to internships and collaborations. [`mail(1)`](mailto:9610hemant@gmail.com) is the fastest way to reach me.
